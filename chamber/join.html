NP Membership

For nonprofit organizations with no membership fee.

Benefits include:

  • Access to selected chamber events
  • Community networking opportunities
  • Business and nonprofit resources

Cost: Free

Bronze Membership

For businesses starting to connect with the local community.

Benefits include:

  • Access to networking events
  • Member directory listing
  • Selected training opportunities

Cost: S/ 150 annually

Silver Membership

For businesses ready to increase their visibility.

Benefits include:

  • All Bronze membership benefits
  • Discounts on selected chamber events
  • Priority access to business workshops
  • Eligibility for a home page spotlight

Cost: S/ 300 annually

Gold Membership

For established businesses seeking broader opportunities.

Benefits include:

  • All Silver membership benefits
  • Featured home page spotlight opportunities
  • Special event invitations
  • Enhanced promotional opportunities
  • Priority access to selected training

Cost: S/ 500 annually
